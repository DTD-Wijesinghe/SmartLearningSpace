# SmartLearningSpace interface redesign

The public pages use an editorial layout with one primary action, a real view of each role’s workflow, and answers about registration and enrollment. The authenticated workspace brings the next action, current course data, and account management into separate sections.

## Research and design decisions

- Canvas makes courses directly accessible from the dashboard and changes what appears based on the user’s role. SmartLearningSpace uses searchable course cards and separate navigation for teaching, learning, and administration. [Canvas dashboard guide](https://community.instructure.com/en/kb/articles/664620-how-do-i-use-the-dashboard)
- Khan Academy gives learners a home base for their work and progress. SmartLearningSpace puts actual enrollment and completion on the course card with a direct route back to the course. [Khan Academy student dashboard](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/46935588089997-How-do-I-use-the-student-dashboard-in-Khan-Academy)
- Duolingo’s path makes the next learning action easy to find. SmartLearningSpace applies that principle through clear enrollment, continuation, and lesson-completion actions. [Duolingo home screen redesign](https://blog.duolingo.com/new-duolingo-home-screen-design/)
